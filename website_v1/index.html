FAQ

Questions from operators, risk and legal teams.

Something specific to your integration? Email the team at team@0nstack.io.

Is ØnStack licensed by BACEN?+

Yes — via acquisition. ØnStack operates under a BACEN-authorized FX institution acquired through an M&A path, which is what lets us be live in 3 months instead of the 18–24 month greenfield licensing queue. We operate under Resolutions 519 / 520 / 521 and the new SPSAV framework for crypto-as-FX.

How is the FX rate guaranteed at quote time?+

Each quote is hedged in real time via our NDF desk. The rate is locked to the client regardless of market movement during settlement. Same mechanism Wise and Western Union use on the retail side — we've operationalized it for stablecoin corridors and made it API-callable.

Which stablecoins and chains do you support?+

USDC (Ethereum, Stellar, Base), USDT (Tron, Ethereum), and BRLA (Polygon). EURC and PYUSD in the roadmap. BTC and ETH are accepted on the Exchange Hub via auto-hedge conversion, never as settlement asset.

What does integration look like?+

REST + webhooks, with a sandbox environment and Postman collection. Typical fintech goes live in 7–14 business days. KYB onboarding is the critical path — usually 5 business days — and we run it in parallel with engineering integration.

How do you handle Travel Rule and AML?+

Travel Rule is compiled into the payout path (TRISA + IVMS-101). AML screening runs on both sender and beneficiary in real time using a COAF-calibrated ruleset plus FATF sanctions lists. You inherit the compliance stack by integrating — no separate vendor to procure.

What's the pricing model for partners?+

Base take rate is 0.80% on RemitFX, 1.00% on iGamingRails, and 0.60% on Exchange Hub institutional flow. Volume breaks kick in at $1M / $10M / $50M monthly. We share a revenue model on request — ask us for the pricing tear-sheet during the sandbox call.
